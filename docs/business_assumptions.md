# Business Assumptions

This project evaluates sales performance by measuring not only revenue, but also the long-term value of the customers acquired by each sales representative.

The following business assumptions were defined before the analysis began.

---

# Customer Acquisition

A customer is considered acquired when their **first successful payment** is recorded.

Invoices without a payment date are excluded because the acquisition date cannot be determined reliably.

---

# Evaluation Period

Only customers whose **first successful payment** occurred between:

- November 1, 2025
- January 31, 2026

are included in the performance evaluation.

Customers acquired before this period are excluded, even if they generated revenue during the selected months.

---

# Revenue Attribution

Only the **first successful payment** is considered acquisition revenue.

Future invoices belong to the customer's Lifetime Value (LTV), not to acquisition revenue.

---

# Salesperson Attribution

A customer is attributed to the sales representative responsible for the **latest Won opportunity before the customer's first payment**.

This rule prevents incorrect attribution when an account contains multiple opportunities or multiple sales representatives.

---

# Opportunity Selection Rules

Only opportunities that satisfy all of the following conditions are considered:

- Stage = Won
- Actual Close Date is available
- Close Date occurred before or on the customer's first payment
- The most recent qualifying opportunity is selected

---

# Currency Standardization

Invoices exist in multiple currencies.

To allow fair comparison, every invoice is converted to USD using the latest available exchange rate prior to the payment date.

---

# Lifetime Value (LTV)

Customer Lifetime Value represents the sum of every paid invoice generated by the customer.

LTV is calculated after currency conversion to USD.

---

# Sales Performance KPIs

Sales representatives are evaluated using four business metrics:

- Customers Acquired
- Initial Revenue
- Total Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Average Customer Lifetime Value

These KPIs provide a more complete evaluation than revenue alone.
