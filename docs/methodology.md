# Analysis Methodology

This project follows a structured business analytics workflow designed to answer a real business question:

> **Who are the company's best sales representatives?**

Instead of measuring sales performance using revenue alone, the analysis evaluates customer acquisition quality through Lifetime Value (LTV).

---

# Step 1 — Understand the Business Problem

The business wants to identify which sales representatives generate the greatest long-term value.

Simply ranking salespeople by revenue can be misleading because:

- Some representatives receive larger accounts.
- Customers generate revenue over time.
- One customer may have multiple sales opportunities.
- Revenue exists in multiple currencies.

Before building any dashboard, these business rules needed to be defined.

---

# Step 2 — Data Quality Assessment

The first step was validating the reliability of the available data.

The audit included:

- Paid invoices without payment dates
- Missing account identifiers
- Available currencies
- Exchange rate availability
- Accounts with multiple sales opportunities

This validation ensured that unreliable records were excluded before the analysis.

---

# Step 3 — Define Business Rules

Several business rules were established before calculating performance metrics.

### Customer Acquisition

A customer is considered acquired when the first successful payment is received.

### Evaluation Period

Only customers whose first payment occurred between:

- November 1, 2025
- January 31, 2026

were included.

### Currency Standardization

Every invoice was converted to USD using the latest available exchange rate before the payment date.

### Sales Attribution

Each customer was assigned to the salesperson responsible for the latest **Won** opportunity before the customer's first payment.

---

# Step 4 — Customer Lifetime Value

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) was calculated by summing every paid invoice generated by each customer after currency normalization.

This metric represents the long-term value of every acquired customer.

---

# Step 5 — Sales Performance Analysis

The final analysis combines acquisition and customer quality through four business KPIs:

- Customers Acquired
- Initial Revenue
- Total Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Average Customer Lifetime Value

Using multiple KPIs provides a more complete evaluation than revenue alone.
